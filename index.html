Why is an area flagged?

Scoring method

Demonstration-only weighted indicators: household-to-meter coverage gap (40%), low billed kWh per meter (20%), feeder technical-loss anomaly (15%), AMI irregularity rate (15%), and survey completeness (10%). Missing inputs are excluded and scores flagged incomplete. Hazard exposure is reported separately, not treated as theft evidence.

Help / How to Use

Simple steps for using the public demonstration and your actual private GIS engineering package.

Navigate the website

1. DashboardSee sample indicators and explanations.
2. Smart mapChoose heatmap, feeder/transformer views or timeline animation.
3. Engineering modulesUse demonstration QC, loss calculator, energy balance, CIM and network topology.
4. Data importsDownload CSV templates, validate aggregate data and import small GeoJSONs.
5. Private portalDownload the full V6 ZIP from ChatGPT, extract and open its HTML to see the actual GIS model.

Do you need Python?

No: for browsing maps, feeder reports, transformer points and all standalone HTML pages.

Yes, optionally: when a team gives you new Shapefiles, GeoPackages, DEM/flood/population rasters, or you want to rebuild GIS-to-transformer reports.

For a new transformer master, the private ZIP contains REBUILD_TRANSFORMER_REPORTS.bat. It recalculates audit CSVs, not the existing fixed HTML snapshot.

Avoid uploading any restricted feeder or customer information to a public website. For a real enterprise multiuser platform, arrange authorised hosting, PostGIS and roles/approvals.

What more information is needed?

Power flowConductor R/X, approved transformer impedance and loss, actual source/switch state and time-aligned demand.
AMI and billingVerified active meters, tariff/status and readings linked to actual transformer service boundaries.
Hazards and populationActual census/WorldPop, flood/DEM and field condition data.
CIM / government systemVerified permanent IDs, CIM profile, electric terminals, database and audit trail.

Understand the common words

Feeder nameUse familiar names such as Rail Bazar or Montgomery. Internal feeder codes are used only for matching files.
Transformer IDThe equipment number used to link GIS, transformer losses and meter records.
Map ZoneA fictional grid patch in the earlier sample map, not a real locality. My Data shows your uploaded feeder names instead.
