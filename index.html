Ranks areas for checking database completeness, missing meters and surveys. It does not identify criminals or establish illegal consumption.

Why is an area flagged?

Select an area from the ranking to view its individual indicators and recommended verification.

Scoring method

Demonstration-only weighted indicators: household-to-meter coverage gap (40%), low billed kWh per meter (20%), feeder technical-loss anomaly (15%), AMI irregularity rate (15%), and survey completeness (10%). Missing inputs are excluded and scores flagged incomplete. Hazard exposure is reported separately, not treated as theft evidence.

External environmental data

AMI & growth screening

Field investigation steps

1. Confirm the meter and spatial boundaries.
2. Check billing interval, vacant and shared premises.
3. Check mapping completeness and measured technical losses.
4. Request authorised field verification if inconsistencies persist.
